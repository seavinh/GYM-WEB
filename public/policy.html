Official App Policy

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

FitLife Gym ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Gym Management System (FitLife GMS) and the FitLife Mobile App (Package ID: com.seavinh.gym). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard user information when you use our mobile application and web portal.

2. Information Collection & Usage

To provide gym membership services, check-in functionality, and workout tracking, we collect limited personal data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and member ID required for gym membership identification.
  • Attendance Data: Check-in and check-out timestamps when scanning membership QR codes.
  • Device & Camera Permission: The app requests camera permission solely to scan QR codes for quick gym entry. No photo or video footage is recorded or uploaded.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We take data protection seriously. All data transmitted between the mobile application, web portal, and backend server is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption protocols. Access tokens are stored securely on the device using encrypted storage.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

FitLife Gym does NOT sell, trade, or rent user personal data to any third-party advertisers or commercial entities. Data is strictly processed for internal gym administration and member account management.

5. Member Gym Rules & Etiquette

  • Clean athletic footwear and proper workout clothing must be worn at all times.
  • Members must re-rack weights and sanitize equipment after each set.
  • Cardio machines are subject to a 30-minute maximum limit during peak facility hours (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM).
  • Memberships are non-transferable. Scanning non-members is strictly prohibited.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Members have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data at any time. You can request deletion by submitting a request through the app settings or by contacting gym administration at support@seavinh.com.
